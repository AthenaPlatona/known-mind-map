# The Known Mind & Society

An illustrated map of what psychology and sociology know with confidence.

Popular books, social media, and news are full of fascinating findings, but many don't hold up when tested again. This map shows only findings backed by large reviews of many studies (meta-analyses), repeat studies run by many labs, or large real-world experiments. They are chosen because they help people make better decisions, avoid common errors, and stay curious and creative in an AI world.

- **Click any picture** to read what the finding means, the key numbers, its limits and its sources.
- **Click Explore** to land on a random finding.
- **Here Be Myths** lists popular claims that didn't hold up.
