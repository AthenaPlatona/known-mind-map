The Known Mind & Society

A map of what psychology and sociology know with confidence. It shows findings that sharpen judgment, curb errors, or spark curiosity and creativity, and only those backed by many studies: large reviews that combine many studies (meta-analyses), repeat studies run by many labs, or large real-world experiments. Each finding is a picture; click one to read what it means, the key numbers, its limits and its sources.
